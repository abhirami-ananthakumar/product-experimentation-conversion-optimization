\# Business Recommendations



\## 8.1 Prioritize the Ad Strategy



The Ad campaign is the stronger-performing strategy based on the observed conversion results.



\- Ad conversion rate: \*\*2.55%\*\*

\- PSA conversion rate: \*\*1.79%\*\*

\- Relative conversion lift: \*\*43.09%\*\*



The business should prioritize the Ad strategy while continuing to monitor campaign efficiency and customer response.



\## 8.2 Optimize Ad Exposure



Higher ad exposure levels are associated with higher observed conversion rates.



The business should investigate effective exposure levels and avoid assuming that continuously increasing ad frequency will always improve performance.



Further controlled experiments should be conducted to identify an optimal exposure range.



\## 8.3 Optimize Campaign Timing



Conversion rates vary across different days and hours.



The analysis indicates opportunities to optimize campaign scheduling based on periods with stronger observed conversion performance.



Campaign timing should be tested through controlled experiments before making large-scale changes.



\## 8.4 Improve Creative Content



The stronger performance of the Ad group suggests that the advertising strategy has potential to improve customer conversion.



The business should continue testing:



\- Ad creatives

\- Messaging

\- Calls to action

\- Campaign formats



A/B testing should be used to identify the most effective combinations.



\## 8.5 Continue Controlled A/B Testing



The current experiment provides statistically significant evidence that the Ad group performs better than the PSA group.



However, additional experiments should be conducted to validate the result across:



\- Different customer segments

\- Different campaign periods

\- Different creative formats

\- Different exposure levels



This will support more reliable long-term optimization.



\## 8.6 Monitor Campaign Cost and Efficiency



Higher conversion does not automatically mean higher profitability.



The business should monitor campaign economics alongside conversion performance, including:



\- Advertising spend

\- Cost per acquisition

\- Revenue

\- Profitability

\- Return on advertising spend



This will help balance conversion improvements with overall marketing efficiency.



\## 8.7 Recommendation Priority



| Priority | Recommendation | Business Impact |

|---|---|---|

| High | Prioritize Ad strategy | Improve customer conversion |

| High | Optimize ad exposure | Improve exposure efficiency |

| High | Continue A/B testing | Support data-driven decisions |

| Medium | Optimize campaign timing | Improve campaign performance |

| Medium | Improve creative content | Increase engagement and conversion |

| Medium | Monitor campaign cost | Balance conversion gains with efficiency |



\## 8.8 Final Business Conclusion



The analysis indicates that the \*\*Ad campaign is the stronger-performing strategy\*\*.



With a conversion rate of \*\*2.55% versus 1.79%\*\*, the Ad group achieved approximately \*\*43.09% relative conversion lift\*\* over the PSA group.



The statistical significance test confirms that this difference is unlikely to be due to random variation alone.



Therefore, the business should \*\*prioritize the Ad strategy\*\* while continuing controlled experimentation to optimize campaign timing, creative content, ad exposure, and overall marketing efficiency.



\## 8.9 Analytical Note



The relationship between ad exposure and conversion should be interpreted carefully.



Higher ad exposure is associated with higher observed conversion rates, but this analysis alone does not establish that increasing ad exposure causes higher conversion.



Further controlled experiments are recommended before making decisions about increasing customer ad exposure.

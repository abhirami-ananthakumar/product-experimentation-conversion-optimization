# A/B Test Results


## Experiment Comparison

The experiment compares users exposed to advertisements (Ad group) with users shown a public service announcement (PSA control group).


## Group Performance

| Metric | Ad Group | PSA Group |

|---|---|---|

| Users | 564,577 | 23,524 |

| Conversions | 14,423 | 420 |

| Conversion Rate | 2.5547% | 1.7854% |


## Conversion Rate Difference

The Ad group achieved a higher conversion rate than the PSA group.

- \*\*Absolute conversion difference:\*\* 0.7692 percentage points

- \*\*Relative conversion lift:\*\* 43.09%

This means the observed conversion rate in the Ad group was approximately 43.09% higher relative to the PSA control group.


## Experiment Outcome

The Ad group was the better-performing experiment group based on observed conversion rate.

However, the observed difference alone does not establish whether the improvement is statistically reliable. Statistical significance testing is documented separately in `statistical\_results.md`.


## Business Interpretation

The initial A/B comparison indicates that the advertising treatment has a positive effect on observed conversion performance.

The next question is whether this difference is statistically significant and large enough to support a business decision.
